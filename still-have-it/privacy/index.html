Privacy

Your card is made in your browser. We don't receive or store its personalized message or generated image. You choose whether to save it or hand it to another app to share. We use a cookie to remember your age confirmation and, if you choose, another to remember your display preferences. We don't need your name, email address, or contacts.

Clear cookies in your browser settings anytime. Remember my choices starts off unless you already opted in with a valid preference cookie.

What stays on your device

Personalized invitation text and the PNG card are created locally in your browser. Sharing uses your device’s share sheet or download. The app you choose to send with has its own practices for the message after you hand it over.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most hosts, Cloudflare may keep ordinary request and security logs (for example IP address, URL, user agent, and timestamps) for operations and abuse prevention, according to Cloudflare’s practices and retention. We do not add analytics or advertising scripts on this cantrip.

Age

Still Have It asks “Are you 13 or older?” with Yes / No. A No answer does not unlock sharing or saving. We do not collect a birth date.
